# Double-entry basics

Every transaction affects at least two accounts. Debits and credits mean left and right ledger sides; they are not synonyms for increase and decrease. Assets and expenses normally increase with debits. Liabilities, equity, and revenue normally increase with credits. Each journal must balance.

Example: contribute $100.00 cash to a new business:

| Account        |  Debit | Credit |
| -------------- | -----: | -----: |
| Cash (asset)   | 100.00 |        |
| Owner's equity |        | 100.00 |

The system stores amounts to four fractional digits and compares exact integer units.
